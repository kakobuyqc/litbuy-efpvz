03 / Litbuy spreadsheet guide

A product list is only useful when the details line up.

Use this Litbuy shopping guide to make each saved link easier to assess. Keep observations separate from seller claims and leave uncertain information marked as unknown.

  1. Choose the role before the item

    Decide whether you need an everyday layer, training jersey, coordinated set, or statement accessory. A defined purpose makes unrelated finds easier to leave out.

  2. Save the exact listing and variant

    Record the original product URL, selected color, size, seller name, and date checked. Similar photographs can appear in listings with different specifications.

  3. Compare measurements and evidence

    Match garment measurements to something you own. Review available detail photos and note whether they show the exact variant you selected.

  4. Review the full cost before paying

    Separate the listed item price from delivery, service charges, currency conversion, and any applicable taxes or duties. Recheck changing information at checkout.

04 / Compare by category

Different finds need different checks.

Shoes: look beyond the size label

Check the listed sizing system, foot-length guidance, and available width information. Compare those details with footwear that already fits you.

Clothing: measure the silhouette

For hoodies, jackets, T-shirts, and jerseys, compare chest width, length, and sleeves. For pants and shorts, add waist, rise, and inseam.

Accessories: inspect the small details

Record dimensions, closures, adjustment range, and stated materials. For sets, confirm which pieces are included and whether sizing applies to every piece.

05 / Straight answers

Before you follow a product link.

Common questions about Litbuy finds, comparison sheets, and the role of this page.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-link shortlist used to organize shopping research. Useful entries include the original listing URL, category, selected variant, measurements, listed price, and the date checked. A spreadsheet does not verify a seller or guarantee product quality.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and education page. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS. It does not represent Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check the units, measurement method, and selected variant. A size label alone does not establish fit.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Record the item price separately from shipping, service charges, currency conversion, and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the final amount before paying.

Are the pictured products available through CAFANS?

The pictures are editorial style references. They do not confirm CAFANS stock, seller authorization, authenticity, or current prices. Open the relevant category and review the actual listing.
